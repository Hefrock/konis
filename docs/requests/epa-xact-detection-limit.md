Subject: Questions about the IAAAP Response Data Viewer (Xact lead results)

Hello,

I maintain an independent, public archive of the information EPA has published about the
25 November 2026 lead azide detonation at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. To describe the
Xact lead results on the IAAAP Response Data Viewer accurately, could you clarify:

**Monitoring status**
1. Is Xact monitoring still running? The public layer has shown no new readings since
   27 November at about 13:21 UTC (7:21 a.m. CST). If monitoring has ended, on what date did
   it end, and will the remaining results be published?
2. The layer also had gaps of about 10 and 16 hours on 26-27 November before readings
   resumed. Were those instrument downtime, or are results uploaded in batches that may
   still arrive?

**Interpreting the values**
3. What is the detection limit (or reporting limit) for lead in these readings? Does a
   value of 0 mean "below the detection limit"?
4. What averaging or sampling interval does each reading represent?
5. Is `Mon_Date_Time` recorded in UTC, and does it mark the start or the end of the
   sampling interval?
6. Are these results preliminary? May they later be revised or replaced by validated data?
7. The Army's 24 November public advisory cites 0.18 µg/m³ as an "air sampling method
   detection limit". Does that figure apply to the Xact readings, and if not, which
   method does it refer to?

**Other results**
8. When does EPA expect to publish the results of the air-filter, soil, dust-wipe and water
   sampling described in the project's QAPP, and where will they appear?

The viewer displays concentrations to one decimal place, so readings such as 0.01 µg/m³
appear as 0.0. Knowing the detection limit would help readers interpret those values
correctly.
