## Literature (T2) noted 25 Nov
- Open burning of artillery propellants: ~60% of lead mass balance deposited to soil
  (WIT Press, AIR12024FU1).
- Aerostat sampling of OB/OD: soil-covered detonations raise PM emission factors
  (J. Hazard. Mater., S0304389414008504).
- No study found supporting a specific post-event monitoring duration; resuspension studies
  run seasons to decades (urban soils, former smelters, mine waste).
