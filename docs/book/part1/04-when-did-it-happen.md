## What we believed

The brief said 09:00 CST; some news said 9 a.m. ET. TODO: how the two readings change which Xact values count as before or after.

## What we found

A seismometer and infrasound sensor 37 km away gave an origin of about 15:00:32 UTC (ground motion 15:00:38.5), checked against the speed of sound. The Army later confirmed about 9:00 CST. The Army's earliest advisories had said 20 Nov at noon. TODO: one figure of the two arrivals.

## What it taught

One station is weak evidence; physics (the two arrivals agreeing with the speed of sound) made it convincing. An archive that keeps every version shows that the plan moved.
