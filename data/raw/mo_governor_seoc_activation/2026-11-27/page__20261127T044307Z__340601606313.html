Governor Kehoe Activates State Emergency Operation Center in Preparation for Planned Detonation at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant

November 24, 2026
Jefferson City

Today, Governor Mike Kehoe activated the State Emergency Operation Center (SEOC) ahead of the U.S. Army’s controlled detonation of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP) in Middletown, Iowa.

The U.S. Army has confirmed that the controlled detonation at the IAAAP will take place Wednesday, November 25, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. This federal operation is intended to reduce the risk of an uncontrolled explosion and is expected to have minimal impact on portions of northeast Missouri.

“While the anticipated impact to Missouri is expected to be minimal, my administration’s focus remains on ensuring Missourians are informed, prepared, and protected,” said Governor Kehoe. “Activating the State Emergency Operations Center is a proactive measure to ensure all resources are available if necessary. State agencies will continue to take the necessary actions to protect the health and safety of our citizens and livestock.” 

State agencies are in contact with environmental and health officials in Iowa and with federal officials, and are ready to respond if lead dust from the detonation reaches the state.

Missourians looking for official information, guidance, and links to resources from state and federal agencies should visit the Missouri Department of Natural Resources' webpage. Updates will continue to be made to the webpage prior to and following the detonation.
